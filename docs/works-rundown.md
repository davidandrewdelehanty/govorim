*Иуда Искариот and Жизнь Василия Фивейского came off this list on 30 August — both now have recordings, found by screening YouTube for uploads whose captions are a real Russian transcription rather than an auto-translation. The same screener works for anything below.*

*The most efficient wins, if you want them: the 27 works with English and no audio need only a YouTube playlist each. The 15 Chekhov/Tolstoy/Gogol short stories in that state are usually single-video readings — easy to find. The Pushkin poems (14 with audio) need only public-domain verse translations. Горький's «Двадцать шесть и одна» and Толстой's «Семейное счастие» are public domain and shouldn't be on the no-English shelf at all.*
