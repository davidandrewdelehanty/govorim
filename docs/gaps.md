# Библиотека — what's still missing

**71 of the library's 171 works are missing something.** The other 100 are complete: Russian text, English translation, and a recording.

The shape of the problem has inverted. Before the audio hunt, 50 works had English but no recording; that is now **8**. What remains is overwhelmingly **translation-sourcing**, not audio-sourcing.

| Суламифь | Куприн А.И. | novella | 1 of 13 verified — wrong or partial reading |
| Кирджали | Пушкин А.С. | short story | 1 of 2 verified |
| Честный вор · Крокодил · Мужик Марей | Достоевский Ф.М. | story collection | bundled entry; no recording is titled this way |

Every one of these had a candidate that *failed verification* rather than none at all — so they need a different recording, not a first one.

**The plays share a cause worth fixing.** The chapter splitter treats speeches as chapters, which is why Ревизор shows 69 "chapters" and Власть тьмы 111. Correcting that splitting would likely let their recordings verify without any new search.
