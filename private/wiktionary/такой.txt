=====Usage notes=====
* See {{m|ru|тако́е|id=pronoun}} for additional pronoun senses specific to the neuter singular.
* Even when used in the plural, if {{m|ru|кто}} follows, the remaining clause is in the singular: {{m|ru|'''таки́е''' (plural), [[кто]] [[лю́бит]] (singular)|tr=takíje, kto ljúbit|t=those (plural) who love (singular)}}.
* When {{m|ru|кто}} is used after {{m-self|ru|тако́й}}, {{m-self|ru|тако́й}} is implied to mean a person or people: {{m|ru|'''таки́е''', [[кто]] [[лю́бит]] [[сноубо́рдинг]]|t=those (people) who love snowboarding}}. {{m|ru|который|Кото́рый}} is used after {{m-self|ru|тако́й}} when {{m-self|ru|тако́й}} refers to a particular noun that has been mentioned (which could still be a category of people, or even the word "people", {{m|ru|лю́ди}}): {{m|ru|[[что]] [[каса́ется]] [[терапе́втов]], [[есть]] '''таки́е''', [[кото́рые]] [[не]] [[помога́ют]]|t=when it comes to therapists (a particular noun), there are some (of that noun) that don't help}}. {{m|ru|что|Что}} after {{m-self|ru|тако́й}} can also be used in either case but may be seen as archaic or colloquial.
