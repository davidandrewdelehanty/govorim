====Usage notes====
* The preposition '''из-за''' in the sense "because of" has a similar connotation with the preposition {{m|ru|по}} (with a dative noun). However, each of them has a different sense. For example:
:* {{ux|ru|[[Аро́н]] [[вчера́]] [[опоздать|опозда́л]] '''и́з-за''' [[пробка|про́бки]].|Aaron was late yesterday '''because of''' a traffic jam.}}
:: (The traffic jam was the cause of Aaron being late. But he was fully aware that the traffic jam was the cause. He actually experienced the traffic jam, and he could blame it for his tardiness.)
:* {{ux|ru|[[Аро́н]] [[вчера́]] [[опоздать|опозда́л]] '''по''' [[рассеянность|рассе́янности]].|Aaron was late yesterday '''because of''' a distraction.}}
:: (The distraction was the cause of Aaron being late. But he was ''not'' fully aware that the distraction was the cause. His situation was as if the distraction itself actively influenced him to become late, but he didn't notice the distraction that well.)
