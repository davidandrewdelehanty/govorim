====Usage notes====
In Russian, the conjunction {{l|ru|е́сли}} can only be used to express a condition or hypothetical situation, while the conjunction {{l|ru|ли}} is used to express the meaning of ''whether''. This is different from English, where ''if'' can be used for both.
