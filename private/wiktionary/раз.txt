===Etymology===
{{inh+|ru|orv|разъ|t=strike}}, from {{inh|ru|sla-pro|*razъ}}. Akin to {{m|ru|рази́ть}}, {{m|ru|ре́зать}}. For a different semantic shift, compare {{cog|pl|raz}}.

Compare typologically {{m+|sla-pro|*brojiti}}, {{m|sla-pro|*brojь}} (akin to {{m|sla-pro|*briti}}, whence {{m|ru|брить}}, << {{m+|ine-pro|*bʰreyH-}}). Also compare {{m+|en|tally}} (<< {{m+|la|tāliō}}, which is a distant source of {{m|ru|та́лия}}).
