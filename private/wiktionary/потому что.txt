====Usage notes====
* The coordinate question is usually served by {{l|ru|почему́}}.
* Unlike English {{m|en|because}}, Russian '''потому что''' can never govern an introductory clause (e.g. English "''Because I was early,'' I had to wait"). For that, {{m|ru|поско́льку}}, {{m|ru|та́к как}} and other conjunctions must be used. This separation of usage is not general to Russian conjunctions and is rather a peculiarity of a few including this one.
