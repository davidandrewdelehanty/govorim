# The hundred most-read Russian works

Ranked roughly by how widely they are actually read — the Russian school
programme first, since that is what "popular" means to a Russian reader,
then the works that carry abroad. Generated by `tools/top100/build.py`;
the machine-readable version is `top-100-russian-works.csv`.

- **55 of 100** are already in the library.
- **82** have a free text on Russian Wikisource. The other 18 are still in
  copyright in Russia; no text link is given for those, on purpose.
- The export service dropped FB2 — it now serves EPUB (all 82 links checked,
  all return `application/epub+zip`). `ebook-convert book.epub book.fb2`
  gives the reader what it parses.
- **17** audio links are marked as partial or uncertain — the running time
  does not match what the text's length says a full reading should cost.
