Which video boundaries can be trusted

Every book whose chapters are cut out of one long video, checked without watching a frame of it. Where one chapter’s figure jumps, its start or end time is in the wrong place — and the direction says which way: above the book’s pace means the segment is too short for its text, below means it is too long.
